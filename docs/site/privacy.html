Covey Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 10, 2026

Covey ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile app that picks one social spot per week for your city and lets you RSVP with your community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Covey, we collect:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided through Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In when you create an account.
  • City preference — the city you select for weekly spot notifications.
  • RSVP responses — whether you mark yourself as Going, Maybe, or Skip for a weekly spot.
  • Device push token — a token used to deliver push notifications to your device. We do not use this for any purpose other than sending you Covey notifications.

We do not collect your precise location. City preference is selected manually by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To identify your account and keep it secure.
  • To send you a weekly push notification and email when your city's spot is announced.
  • To display RSVP counts to other users in your city (counts only — your name is not shown).
  • To remember your city preference across sessions.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Covey uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services (SES) — email delivery. AWS Privacy Policy
  • Google Places API — venue information (name, address, rating). We do not share your personal data with Google Places. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In — authentication only. We receive only the name and email you choose to share.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. RSVP history is retained to display past spots. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days.

5. Push Notifications and Email

Covey sends one push notification and one email per week when your city's spot is announced. You can turn off push notifications at any time in your device's notification settings. To stop emails, contact us at hello@coveyspot.app.

6. Children's Privacy

Covey is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@coveyspot.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or by email. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@coveyspot.app.
